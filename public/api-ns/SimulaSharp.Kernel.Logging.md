Structured logging. A component's [`ILogger`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Logging.ILogger) stamps each message with three timestamps (sim, mission, wall), an issuer (the component's full dotted path), a level, and the call site, producing a [`LogEntry`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Logging.LogEntry) that fans out through a [`LogRouter`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Logging.LogRouter) to every registered [`ILogSink`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Logging.ILogSink).
