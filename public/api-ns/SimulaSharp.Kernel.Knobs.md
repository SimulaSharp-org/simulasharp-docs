The observation-and-control channel. A [`Knob`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Knobs.Knob) is a named readable value, settable value, or trigger on a model instance; the generator discovers them and an [`IKnobChannel`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Knobs.IKnobChannel) (the MMI) receives them and their changes.

See [Observability](#/guide/observability) and [the knob attributes](#/guide/attribute-reference).
