Global events: scheduler-less, multi-producer pub/sub that any model or service may subscribe to or trigger. [`SimEvent`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Events.SimEvent) / [`SimEvent<T>`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Events.SimEvent%601) are the stock events: a trigger posts each subscriber's handler onto that subscriber's own site. [`ISimEvent<TSub>`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Events.ISimEvent%601) / [`ISimEvent<TSub, T>`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Events.ISimEvent%602) are the full surface for a custom (routing) event, and [`ISimEventInvocator`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Events.ISimEventInvocator) / [`ISimEventInvocator<T>`](#/api/type/SimulaSharp.Kernel.Events.ISimEventInvocator%601) the trigger-only face an `[EventReference]` member holds.

See [Global events](#/guide/events) and the assembly's `Events:` / `Subscriptions:` / `Triggers:` blocks in [the YAML reference](#/guide/assembly-yaml-reference).
